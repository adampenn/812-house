812 House sits at the corner of West 1st & Cherry — about as close to Chico State as off-campus housing gets. Roll out of bed and be in class in five minutes, hit the WREC without moving your car, and walk home from downtown on the weekends.

Four units, all with their own front door

Two 2-bedroom units and two 1-bedroom units with the kind of space you don't get in a complex — real living rooms, separate dining rooms, and covered porches. Every unit comes with one off-street parking spot in the back lot (two shorter cars can fit tandem). Contact us for current pricing and availability.
